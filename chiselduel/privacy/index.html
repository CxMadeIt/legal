Chisel Duel! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

The short version: Chisel Duel! has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress, coins, gems and statues stay on your device.

1. Information we handle

Game progress. Your player name, trophies, coins, gems, boosters, skins, statue collection, Chisel Pass progress, match history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.

Races. Rivals are computer-controlled. Playing a race does not send anything off your device.

Purchases. Gem packs and the Starter Pack are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; Apple handles them under its own privacy policy.

Sharing. When you tap Share on the results screen, the image and message go wherever you choose through Apple's share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

What we do NOT collect: your email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone, advertising identifiers, or any analytics that track you within the app or across apps.

2. Your choices and control

  • To erase your game data, open the Profile tab and tap Reset Progress, then confirm. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.
  • There are no accounts to close and nothing on our servers to request deletion of, because nothing leaves your device.
  • A Starter Pack you bought can be restored on a new device from Shop → Restore Purchases. Gems already spent can't be restored.

3. Children

Chisel Duel! is designed for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Changes

If Chisel Duel! ever adds online play with real people, analytics or accounts, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated in the same release, with a new effective date.
