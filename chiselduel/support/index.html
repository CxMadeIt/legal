Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-in. Everything lives on your device.

How do I play?
Tap the block to chisel. Drag to spin it. Hit the glowing yellow cracks to smash bigger chunks, and the red TNT blocks to blast a crater. Reveal the statue before your rival does.

I bought the Starter Pack on another iPhone. How do I get it back?
Open Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?
Profile tab → Reset Progress, or uninstall the app.

Are there ads?
No.
