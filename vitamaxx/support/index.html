Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. VitaMaxx has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything lives on your device.

How do I delete my data?
Open the You tab and tap Delete all my data. That removes every scan, photo and preference from your device immediately. Deleting the app does the same thing.

How accurate are the estimates?
They're AI estimates for general wellness and motivation, not measurements. Portion size, preparation and hidden ingredients all shift real nutrient content. VitaMaxx isn't a medical device and doesn't replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.

Why does a scan sometimes fail?
Scanning needs an internet connection. If the service is busy you'll see a message asking you to try again in a moment — your previous scans are unaffected.

Can I change my water goal?
Yes — the You tab lets you set any daily goal. The default is 64 oz.
