VitaMaxx Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

The short version: VitaMaxx has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your results and progress stay on your device. Photos, and anything you send to Maxx, go securely to Cloudflare Workers AI only to answer that request. Maxx+ purchases are made through Apple and confirmed by RevenueCat. Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train AI models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent.

1. Information we handle

Food, drink and label photos. When you choose a photo scan, the app re-encodes the image, at most 1,600 pixels on its longest side, which leaves out ordinary photo metadata such as location, then transmits it securely to VitaMaxx's Cloudflare Worker and processed by Cloudflare Workers AI solely to estimate nutrient content or water volume, or to read a Nutrition Facts label. VitaMaxx has no user accounts and sends no name, email, account ID, device ID, or advertising ID with the photo. A photo may also be stored locally on your device so you can see your meal history.

Packaged products. When a photo shows a packaged food or drink, VitaMaxx's server sends the product's brand, name, flavor and size, as read from the package, to the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central database to look up the manufacturer's nutrition figures. The photo itself and anything about you are never sent to USDA.

Maxx conversations (Maxx+). Maxx sends nothing until you agree to it. When you use Maxx, what you type, the meal you are working on, and the earlier messages in that conversation are sent securely to VitaMaxx's server and processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to produce a reply. If you choose to give Maxx your age or the sex used for nutrient reference values, those are sent with your questions so answers use the right daily values; they are stored on your device, and you can remove them at any time from Maxx's menu.

Your recent meals (Maxx+). So Maxx can answer questions about what you have eaten, each question also carries the meals you saved in the last 14 days: their names, foods and portions, times, nutrient totals, and each day's water. The app builds this from your device for each question; VitaMaxx's server uses it only to answer that question and does not keep it. Photos are never included. Turn this off at any time with Maxx sees my meals in Maxx's menu.

VitaMaxx's server does not keep your messages. It keeps a one-way fingerprint of each request, so a repeated request is not counted twice, and it keeps Maxx's reply for up to 48 hours so a dropped connection can still receive it. Meal identifiers, with no meal content, are kept for about a week. All of these are deleted automatically.

Purchases. Maxx+ purchases are made through Apple. VitaMaxx uses RevenueCat to confirm them. To do that, VitaMaxx's server issues a random identifier for your installation. It is not your name, email, Apple ID or a device identifier. The app shares that identifier, with your purchase history, with RevenueCat. VitaMaxx's server keeps the identifier for up to a year so it can check whether Maxx+ is active, and keeps counts of how many Maxx requests were used, with no content, for about two months.

Scan results and preferences. Nutrient estimates, water logs, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device. The scan server does not save photos or results to a database, object store, log, or other persistent storage.

What we do NOT collect: your name, email, contacts, location, health records, advertising identifiers, or any analytics that track you across apps.

2. How photos and Maxx requests are processed

Requests are sent over an encrypted connection to a serverless API operated by VitaMaxx on Cloudflare, then processed by a Cloudflare Workers AI model. A photo is used to produce one response and is not written to persistent server storage by VitaMaxx.

What that means in practice:

  • Cloudflare identifies prompts, image submissions, and outputs as customer content. Cloudflare states that it does not use Workers AI customer content to train models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent.
  • VitaMaxx does not place customer content in Cloudflare storage products such as R2, KV, Durable Objects, or Vectorize. The short-lived Maxx records described above are kept in VitaMaxx's own database and deleted on the schedule given.
  • Network providers necessarily receive technical connection data such as an IP address to deliver an internet request, but VitaMaxx does not store, profile, sell, or use that information for advertising or tracking.
  • Only photograph things you are comfortable sharing. Point the camera at your plate or drink, not at people, documents, screens, or private details in the background.

3. Your choices and control

  • You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Delete all my data. This removes every scan, locally stored photo, water log, and preference from your device, and deletes Maxx's cached replies from the server.
  • Delete cached AI responses in Maxx's menu deletes Maxx's cached replies from the server at any time. Remove my details removes the details you gave Maxx, including your age.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Server records expire on their own as described above; because they are not tied to your name or email, we cannot look them up by either.
  • Camera and photo library access are used only when you actively scan and can be managed in iOS Settings.
  • Manage or cancel a Maxx+ subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

4. Children

VitaMaxx is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and be posted at this address with a new effective date.
