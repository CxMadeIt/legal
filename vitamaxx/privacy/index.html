VitaMaxx Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

The short version: VitaMaxx has no accounts and no ads. Your scans, photos and progress stay on your device. Food photos you scan are sent to an AI provider once, only to analyze them, and are not used to identify you.

1. Information we handle

Food and water photos. When you scan a meal or a water bottle, the photo is transmitted securely to our AI processing provider (OpenAI) solely to estimate its nutrient or water content. Photos are not linked to your name or identity — VitaMaxx has no user accounts. A copy of the photo is stored locally on your device so you can see your meal history.

Scan results and preferences. Nutrient estimates, your goals, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.

What we do NOT collect: your name, email, contacts, location, health records, advertising identifiers, or any analytics that track you across apps.

2. How photos are processed

Photos are sent over an encrypted connection to OpenAI's API to generate a nutrient estimate. Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, API data is not used to train their models. We do not retain photos on any server of our own.

3. Your choices and control

  • You can delete all app data at any time in You → Delete all my data. This removes every scan, photo and preference from your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Camera and photo library access are used only when you actively scan and can be managed in iOS Settings.

4. Children

VitaMaxx is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and be posted at this address with a new effective date.
