VitaMaxx Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

The short version: VitaMaxx has no accounts and no ads. Your meal history and progress stay on your device. When you choose to scan a food or water photo, a resized copy is securely processed only to produce your estimate. Customer scans are not shared for AI model training.

1. Information we handle

Food and water photos. When you actively scan a meal or water container, VitaMaxx redraws and resizes the image, which removes ordinary photo metadata, then transmits it securely through our processing provider (Cloudflare) to our AI provider (OpenAI) solely to estimate nutrient or water content. VitaMaxx does not send your name, email, advertising identifier or an account ID with the photo. A copy of a meal photo is stored locally on your device so you can see your meal history.

Scan results and preferences. Nutrient estimates, portions, goals, streaks and settings are stored on your device. Our processing service does not create a VitaMaxx account or permanently store your photo or result.

What we do NOT collect: your name, email, contacts, location, health records, advertising identifiers, or any analytics that track you across apps.

2. How photos are processed

Photos are sent over encrypted connections through Cloudflare to OpenAI's API. OpenAI API data sharing is disabled for VitaMaxx production traffic, so customer photos and results are not used to train OpenAI models. VitaMaxx requests non-persistent responses and does not store photos or results in its processing service.

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless a shorter retention control applies, after which they are deleted under OpenAI's policies. Cloudflare may process limited network information needed to deliver, secure and rate-limit the request. VitaMaxx does not use either provider for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Your choices and control

  • You can delete all app data at any time in You → Delete all my data. This removes every scan, locally saved photo and preference from your device. It does not accelerate the automatic expiration of provider security logs.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Camera and photo library access are used only when you actively scan and can be managed in iOS Settings.

4. Children

VitaMaxx is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and be posted at this address with a new effective date.
