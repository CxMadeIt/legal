Pomo Pomo Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

The short version: Pomo Pomo Arcade has no accounts and no ads. Your game progress, coins and cosmetic purchases stay on your device.

1. Information we handle

Game progress. Levels, coins, streaks and unlocked cosmetics are stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.

Purchases. Cosmetic in-app purchases (themed skin sets and accessories) are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details — Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

What we do NOT collect: your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera access, advertising identifiers, or any analytics that track you across apps.

2. Your choices and control

  • All game data lives locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes every bit of it — progress, coins and cosmetics.
  • There are no accounts to close and nothing stored on our servers to request deletion of, because nothing ever leaves your device.

3. Children

Pomo Pomo Arcade is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and be posted at this address with a new effective date.
