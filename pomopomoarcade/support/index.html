Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Pomo Pomo Arcade has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything lives on your device.

How do I delete my data?
Uninstalling the app removes all game progress, coins and cosmetics immediately — there's nothing stored anywhere else.

Can I lose my progress or get a "game over"?
No. Pomo Pomo Arcade is designed around comfort — no game overs, no timers, and no guilt-trip streak mechanics.

Are there ads?
No, and there never will be. Optional cosmetic purchases support development instead.
